University Undergraduate Attendance and Course Absence Policy

1. Class Attendance Expectations:
Regular class attendance is required of all enrolled students. Instructors establish individual attendance policies, which must be clearly stated in the course syllabus distributed during the first week of classes.

2. Excused Absences:
Absences may be formally excused for documented illness, university-sanctioned athletic or academic travel, religious observances, or verifiable family emergencies. Students seeking an excused absence must notify the instructor via email at least 24 hours prior to scheduled absences, or within 48 hours following an unforeseen emergency.

3. Attendance Thresholds and Course Failure:
Unexcused absences exceeding 20% of scheduled class sessions (e.g., 6 class meetings for a MWF course, or 4 meetings for a TR course) shall result in an administrative withdrawal or an automatic final grade of 'FA' (Failure due to Absence) at the discretion of the department chair.

4. Appeals Procedure:
Students wishing to contest an FA notation must file a formal written petition with the Dean of Students within five (5) business days following the posting of mid-term or final grades.
